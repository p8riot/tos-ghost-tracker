Gameplay Tools Lore
Notes
Hunt heartbeat: hearing a heartbeat during a hunt means the ghost sees you. Break line-of-sight and RUN! Stamina: a full stamina sprint is about 11 steps. Avoid draining the bar completely because regeneration takes longer. A useful rhythm is roughly 10 steps, stop for 3–4 seconds, then sprint again. Revenant permanently reduces a targeted player's stamina by half for the remainder of the contract. Electronics during hunts: the cellphone, including its flashlight, can safely be used during a hunt. Turn off or drop other electronics you are carrying. Favorite room: ghosts have a favorite room, but evidence can appear elsewhere inside the location. The Infrasound Receiver must be in the favorite room to capture Audio evidence. Sanctified Cross: can only be flipped in the ghost's favorite room. Hunt heart-rate threshold: most ghosts can normally hunt at a 100 BPM average team heart rate. Demon, Hupia, and Wiederganger can hunt as early as 77 BPM. The threshold uses the average BPM of players inside the location. Holy Water hunt blocks: special ghosts may temporarily replace their normal hunt cooldown after being sprayed. Demon and Wisp are blocked for 2 minutes; Tariaksuq is blocked for 90 seconds. Check the ghost's Unique Behaviors for its special Holy Water or cooldown rule. Real vs Diminishing Evidence: real evidence reappears several times at full strength. Diminishing evidence may first appear at full strength a couple of times, then appears weaker over time. Freezing can never be Diminishing. Diminishing filtering: on limited-evidence runs, a Diminishing evidence type can also be one of the ghost's assigned evidence types, so it does not rule that ghost out. When the run has all 3 Real Evidence, extra Diminishing evidence can be treated as outside the ghost's three assigned evidence types. Marid evidence exception: Marid can leave extra Diminishing evidence on top of its assigned evidence, including on 0 Real Evidence runs. Forced Evidence: forced evidence applies whenever the run has Real Evidence, regardless of the configured Diminishing Evidence count. It does not apply on 0 Real Evidence runs. Ghost Orbs: normally require infrared vision to see, but an orb can also be photographed using the cellphone camera flash. Sluagh can produce multiple Ghost Orbs. Puca: can imitate behaviors of other ghost types while passive or hunting, but it cannot extinguish candles. Observing a ghost extinguish a candle rules Puca out; other mimicked behavior observations do not. Candles: Can Light is a specific identifying behavior for ghosts whose Notes list it. Cannot Extinguish is also a specific restriction. Puca may mimic candle-lighting behavior, but it still cannot extinguish candles. Main Breaker: this is the main switch that controls the entire breaker box. Echo, Wewe Gombel, and Wisp cannot use the main breaker normally; the automatic breaker change after a hunt is an exception and should not be treated as an identifying interaction. Individual Breakers: these are the room-specific switches inside the breaker box. All ghosts can turn individual breakers off. Echo and Wewe Gombel can only turn individual breakers off; every other ghost can turn individual breakers both on and off. Radio interactions: TVs and alarm clocks do not count as Radio interactions. The Old Tape Recorder and Gramophone do count as Radio interactions. Lights: a light exploding counts as the ghost turning a light off. Player illumination is now dynamic, so being closer to active light sources provides more protection from anxiety than simply standing somewhere in a lit room.
