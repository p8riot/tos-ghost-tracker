Notes
Hearing heartbeat during hunt = Ghost sees you. RUN! Full stamina run will give you 11 steps. Don't consume all stamina or it takes longer to regenerate. Take 10 steps, stop for 3 or 4 seconds, sprint again. Revenant gives permanent debuff to stamina once targeted. Cellphone, including phone flashlight, can safely be used during hunt. Turn off or drop all other electronics you're carrying. Ghosts will have a favorite room but they can leave evidence anywhere inside the location. Infrasound Receiver has to be in favorite room to capture audio. Sanctified Cross can only be flipped in favorite room. Forced hunts from Holy Water (Demon, Tariaksuq, Wisp) execute at the specified time if any players are inside the location. Forced hunts pause the regular hunt cooldown. If no players are inside when timer expires, the forced hunt happens when a player enters the location. The hunt threshold for all ghosts (except Demon) is 100 BPM. This threshold is the average BPM of all players inside the location. Players outside the location with a low heart rate do not help prevent a hunt. TVs and alarm clocks do not count as Radio interaction. Old Tape Recorder and Gramophone do count as Radio interaction. Lights exploding count as lights off.
